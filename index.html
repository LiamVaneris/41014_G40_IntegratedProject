Track A, mobile robot: Within our Mujoco simulation, our Turtlebot3 is equiped with 2 driven wheels, a 2D lidar camera and a front facing camera. The wheel angles are fed into Odometry which works out the distance and rotation of the wheels with a degree of error (see diagram). To account for this error, the front facing camera sees the marker and its range and bearing is recorded. The Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) takes these data points and estimates the error by calculating and appropriate adjustment depending on how confident the filter is on the odometry readings.
